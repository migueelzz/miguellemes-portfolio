## Why I Started Writing

Writing is one of the most powerful tools for organizing thoughts. I've been building software for a few years now and realized that documenting what I learn — not just for others, but for future me — is incredibly valuable.

This blog is a space where I'll share:

- **Technical deep dives** — exploring topics I'm working with day-to-day
- **Project breakdowns** — behind-the-scenes of things I build
- **Lessons learned** — mistakes, discoveries, and things worth knowing

## What to Expect

Posts will vary in length and format. Some will be short notes, others longer explorations. I'm not aiming for perfection — I'm aiming for consistency and honesty.

## Getting Started

If you're reading this, welcome. I hope something I write here is useful to you at some point.

You can always reach me via the links on the homepage — I'd love to hear from you.
